# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

People who train with weights or mixed modalities and need to record a workout quickly while moving between sets, often with unreliable gym connectivity. The primary job is planning, executing, and reviewing training without waiting on a network request.

## Product Purpose

Fitness Goal makes workout data feel immediate and dependable. It supports planned and unplanned sessions, progressive overload, rest timing, exercise history, and body metrics entirely on-device today while preserving a clean path to background synchronization later.

## Positioning

The training log that remains fast, complete, and trustworthy with or without a connection.

## Brand Personality

Focused, capable, and encouraging. The product should feel like well-made training equipment: precise under pressure, quiet when it should be, and satisfying to use repeatedly.

## Anti-references

Avoid gamified neon fitness dashboards, generic stacks of oversized cards, web-style controls ported into a native shell, decorative gradients, and interfaces that hide common logging actions behind modal layers.

## Design Principles

- Keep the active set within one thumb and one decision.
- Show useful training context at the moment it changes a choice.
- Treat offline operation as the normal state, not an error state.
- Prefer familiar native behavior over novelty.
- Make progress legible without turning every screen into a dashboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect Dynamic Type and system font scaling, 44 pt iOS and 48 dp Android touch targets, safe-area and keyboard insets, light and dark appearance, reduced-motion settings, clear focus labels, and color-independent completion states.
